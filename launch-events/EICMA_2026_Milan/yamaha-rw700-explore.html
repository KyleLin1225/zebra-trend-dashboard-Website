Shift inputs at the grip

The left-grip close-up shows multiple raised buttons gathered around the rider’s thumb. Physical separation gives the hand a path to frequent actions without searching a touchscreen. The controls still need glove and vibration tests to prove that similar shapes do not cause wrong inputs.

A red switch stands apart

A bright red switch interrupts otherwise dark handlebar hardware in the detail image. Color and position distinguish this action before the rider reads a label. Its meaning should also remain available through shape and feedback when visibility is poor or color is unreliable.

Display hierarchy

State on the TFT

The instrument photograph places speed, transmission and ride information inside one compact screen. A dominant number can be read quickly while secondary status remains nearby. A rider needs to recognize the active mode before a response changes, particularly while moving between surfaces.

Navigation context

Modes beside the route

The second screen view shows a map and connected information rather than a bare speed readout. 02 · Technology / Product Architecture

Support distinct riding and service states.

Yamaha RW700 EXPLORE · Storage under the seat · www.1000ps.com
Access sequence

Storage under the seat

The open saddle reveals a recessed storage bay immediately below the primary rider position. Using body volume keeps carried items close without a separate rear box. A work adaptation should verify latch reach, wet-item separation and one-handed access in real parking postures.

Yamaha RW700 EXPLORE · A stable service stance · www.1000ps.com
Support hardware

A stable service stance

The underside photograph shows the center stand bearing the parked scooter’s weight. A dedicated physical state changes access to the wheel, drivetrain and lower panels. That stability helps routine checks, but deployment force and ground conditions deserve direct validation.

Yamaha RW700 EXPLORE · Controls around the stem · www.1000ps.com
Control cluster

Controls around the stem

The close-up shows a compact set of controls beside the key and steering axis. A distinct physical cluster can assign setup actions to a predictable location away from motion controls. Its spacing must prevent accidental operation while a rider turns the bars or wears thick gloves.

Yamaha RW700 EXPLORE · Adjustable contact setting · www.1000ps.com
Rider fit

Adjustable contact setting

The detail shows an adjustment dial near the upper front suspension and cockpit. A marked mechanical adjustment gives a rider a repeatable way to tune contact and response. The effect needs a clear setting reference and instruction, rather than an unlabeled range of turns.

Rear wheel and drive

06 · Key Takeaway

Make mode changes physical and unmistakable.

The RW700 EXPLORE combines Y-AMT transmission choices, road and gravel settings, adjustable footrests, heated contact points and a center stand. These are useful for Zebra because workers also change modes while gripping, moving and servicing equipment. Distinct tactile actions, visible state and stable maintenance positions should be tested together under gloves, vibration, cold and contamination.

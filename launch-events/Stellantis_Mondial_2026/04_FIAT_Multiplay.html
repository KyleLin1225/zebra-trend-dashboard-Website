01 · Design Highlights

Useful flexibility starts with architecture rather than accessories.

One footprint holds several everyday roles · FIAT · Official imagery
FAMILY ENVELOPE

One footprint holds several everyday roles

Multiplay combines MPV space, SUV character and pick-up utility in a 4.45-metre concept. Its enclosed family arrangement gives a different role to the same rear volume that can later become an open bed. The concept organises around changing tasks; FIAT explicitly calls it an ideas laboratory rather than a committed production model.

Daylight makes an adaptable room feel connected · FIAT · Official imagery
PANORAMIC CABIN

Daylight makes an adaptable room feel connected

Large windows and a panoramic roof surround Multiplay's cabin, making the seating arrangement visually open to the outside. The generous glazing keeps the concept's family and leisure space connected to its environment rather than reading as a sealed utility compartment. Visual openness supports recreation and shared use, while glare, heat and actual sight lines remain separate ergonomic questions.

A spoiler can become a carrying tool · FIAT · Official imagery
SHARED COMPONENT

A spoiler can become a carrying tool

FIAT describes a rear spoiler that rotates into a carrier for up to two bicycles in pick-up mode. One installed component changes from an aerodynamic role into a practical loading function, reducing dependence on a separate external accessory. The bicycle configuration makes utility visible; load rating and locking feedback remain unpublished, requiring clear operating boundaries.

Adaptable seating keeps occupants close to the activity · FIAT · Official imagery
FAMILY ZONE

Adaptable seating keeps occupants close to the activity

Multiplay explores six seats plus an additional flexible Petsenger element instead of treating the third row as an afterthought. The rear compartment is described as a dedicated family zone that can accommodate children and, when needed, an adult. The arrangement connects flexibility with supervision, while production restraint requirements cannot be inferred from the welcoming concept imagery.

02 · Technology / Product Architecture

A sliding rear module and shared components change the vehicle’s job.

The C-pillar changes the passenger-cargo boundary · FIAT · Official imagery
METAMORFOSI

The C-pillar changes the passenger-cargo boundary

The rear module slides longitudinally on dedicated guides, transforming the enclosed family layout into four seats and an open load bed. Changing the rear enclosure makes cargo capacity an available mode rather than a permanent compromise in passenger space. The visible result is clear; hidden rail sealing, structural latches and pinch protection remain decisive, undisclosed engineering questions.

The floor layout reveals where flexibility is concentrated · Auto Express
CONFIGURATION MAP

The floor layout reveals where flexibility is concentrated

The overhead view shows the seats, centre elements and rear zone within a single cabin footprint. By locating changeable elements together, the architecture suggests a deliberate relationship between occupied space and available carrying space. The configuration can be understood as a system rather than disconnected accessories; the view does not establish precise clearances.

A seat-like module supports several kinds of use · Auto Express
PETSENGER

A seat-like module supports several kinds of use

FIAT describes the Petsenger unit as child-seat mounting space, a pet area, table or storage compartment. Its modular role lets the same part serve occupants and belongings as the journey changes, rather than assigning one fixed purpose to each cabin location. The role is concrete, but future restraint and retention instructions must remain clearer than the module’s playful appearance.

A parked seat turns travel space into rest space · Auto Express
SUNSEAT

A parked seat turns travel space into rest space

The front passenger seat can become a rear-facing lounge using the dashboard as a backrest when the vehicle is parked. This transformation reuses the cabin's existing surfaces to create a leisure posture under the panoramic roof. Rest extends usefulness beyond movement, but the parked-use condition must remain unmistakable rather than suggest a travelling posture.

03 · Aesthetic Analysis

Mediterranean colour and knitted surfaces make transformation feel domestic.

A long colour band makes the technology quieter · Auto Express
HORIZON DASHBOARD

A long colour band makes the technology quieter

Multiplay's horizontal dashboard strip is inspired by the Mediterranean horizon, connecting exterior and interior colour. The display is integrated into this architecture and can disappear from view when the vehicle is switched off, according to FIAT. Digital technology becomes part of the room; the calm off-state still needs essential status to be recognisable when required.

Softness and technical manufacture share one surface · Auto Express
3D KNIT

Softness and technical manufacture share one surface

FIAT specifies monomaterial textile and a no-stitch 3D-knitting process for most soft components. The knitted texture brings a more domestic feel to functional surfaces while the proposed process aims to reduce waste and separate-material complexity. The macro shows tactile character; waste reduction, cleaning and recovery benefits require evidence beyond the proposed material process.

A friendly message belongs where the user touches · Auto Express
SMALL CONTACT DETAIL

A friendly message belongs where the user touches

The Ciao-marked interior detail introduces a small greeting within the brightly coloured seating environment. A concentrated word and contrasting edge give the contact region personality without turning the entire cabin into a graphic surface. The detail adds domestic friendliness, while required changing and locking instructions need a separate, unmistakable visual role.

A repeated grid connects the face to the brand’s setting · Auto Express
LINGOTTO LIGHT

A repeated grid connects the face to the brand’s setting

Multiplay's narrow repeated lighting pattern recalls the illuminated grid of FIAT's historic Lingotto factory, as reported by Autocar. Its linear rhythm stretches across the front and contrasts with the soft, rounded body and cabin shapes. The repeated grid adds urban-Italian character; its graphic reference does not prove lighting performance or off-road capability.

05 · What Zebra Can Apply

Four concrete experiments for Zebra products.

01 · Give every attachment mode one lock cue

Map strap, trigger and cradle configurations around actual task changes, borrowing Metamorfosi's mode-based thinking. Require a distinct tactile or visible locked-state cue, then test incorrect assembly and one-handed switching.

02 · Earn multifunctionality through reduced burden

Prototype a component that serves two compatible accessory roles, inspired by the spoiler-to-bike-carrier idea. Compare carried parts, setup steps and failure recovery before accepting the extra mechanism.

03 · Make dormant technology remain informative

Explore a low-visual-noise dock interface that reduces optional display presence when idle, following the hidden dashboard. Keep charge and fault states readable; test wake-up understanding with first-time users.

04 · Treat soft parts as replaceable systems

Investigate knitted strap or pad constructions with fewer material families, using FIAT's textile approach as a reference. Evaluate sweat, cleaning, abrasion and replacement time before claiming environmental benefit.

06 · Key Takeaway

Multifunctionality is valuable only when every configuration remains understandable.

Multiplay makes flexibility structural through its moving rear module, adaptable seating and spoiler-to-bike-rack transformation. Zebra can apply the same task-led approach to attachment systems, while making locked states, contamination boundaries and service paths easier to recognise than the transformation itself.

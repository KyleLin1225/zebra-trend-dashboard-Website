Alfa Romeo
Cuorerosso

A driver-centred manifesto that connects sculptural mass, moving instruments and tactile controls. Cuorerosso explores Alfa Romeo’s multi-energy future through a coherent physical relationship between driver and machine.

01 · Design Highlights

Sportiness is organised around a person, not a specification sheet.

Long bonnet, compact cabin · Auto Express
PROPORTION

Long bonnet, compact cabin

Cuorerosso stretches a long bonnet ahead of a compact 2+2 cabin, giving the vehicle a clear direction of travel. The low roof and muscular shoulders concentrate visual mass around the wheels rather than the glazing. The silhouette establishes a composed sporting character before badges or performance figures enter the conversation.

A face built from three landmarks · Auto Express
TRILOBO

A face built from three landmarks

The central Scudetto and two headlamps form a contemporary interpretation of Alfa Romeo's Trilobo. The shield grows from the bonnet volume, while deliberately legible lamp proportions give the front a focused expression. This stable three-part arrangement allows surface details to evolve while keeping the front immediately recognisable.

The opening becomes part of arrival · Auto Express
ACCESS

The opening becomes part of arrival

Rear-facing doors expose the red cabin and make entry an unusually visible part of the concept experience. The opening presents seats and controls together, so the interior appears as an immersive destination rather than a hidden compartment. The theatrical entry also raises a practical question: can users discover the roof-mounted touch opening without a conventional handle?

The cabin wraps around the task · Auto Express
DRIVER ENVELOPE

The cabin wraps around the task

Four individual seats sit within longitudinal console and door volumes that visually gather the occupants. The thin dashboard continues into the doors, balancing this enclosing structure with a broad horizontal reference. The enclosure creates sporting intensity, while the transverse line preserves perceived width and a clear orientation reference.

02 · Technology / Product Architecture

Mechanical feedback and selective information work together.

Instruments physically change the information level · Auto Express
CANNOCCHIALE VIVENTE

Instruments physically change the information level

Alfa's Cannocchiale Vivente uses moving instrument housings to switch between two separate displays and one concentrated presentation. The mechanism reduces displayed information as driving engagement increases, turning interface hierarchy into a physical transformation. The system foregrounds essentials as engagement rises; the photograph shows its twin state, while motion is manufacturer-described.

Digital surfaces keep tactile anchors · Auto Express
MAGMA DISPLAY

Digital surfaces keep tactile anchors

The Magma Display combines a digital touch area with a rotary dial, a drive-mode selector and toggle controls. Distinct physical geometries let the fingers locate important inputs while the surface carries changeable information. Distinct shapes preserve tactile localisation inside a digital cockpit; the claimed precision still needs task-based evaluation.

Steer-by-wire does not remove every analogue cue · Auto Express
CON-TATTO VOLANTE

Steer-by-wire does not remove every analogue cue

Cuorerosso explores electronically controlled steering while retaining mechanical controls with haptic and acoustic feedback. The steering control becomes a deliberate contact point where electronic response and physical confirmation are designed together. The principle preserves understandable feedback when a connection becomes indirect, rather than assuming electronics must erase physical confirmation.

A truncated tail ties sculpture to airflow · Auto Express
AERODYNAMIC PACKAGE

A truncated tail ties sculpture to airflow

The truncated rear and body-integrated low diffuser are part of Alfa's aerodynamic approach, alongside uninterrupted surfacing. Rather than adding a conspicuous separate wing, the rear geometry helps organise airflow within the main volume. Alfa states a concept drag coefficient of 0.28; visible integration supports the sculpture, while the figure remains manufacturer-attributed.

03 · Aesthetic Analysis

Liquid red, interrupted light and contact-led materials.

Colour appears to live inside the surface · Auto Express
LIQUID INK RED

Colour appears to live inside the surface

Liquid Ink Red uses a fine aluminium base under a transparent coloured layer, according to Alfa. Light returning through the coating creates depth and shifts the appearance from intense red toward darker tones as the angle changes. Tonal depth amplifies the uninterrupted volumes, making paint participate in perceived shape rather than divide it into decorative zones.

Interrupted light gives a familiar circle new depth · Auto Express
PULSE AND SHADOW

Interrupted light gives a familiar circle new depth

The rear lamp keeps a circular identity while its layered, interrupted structure produces a more dimensional reading. Light and dark segments make the lamp appear as a designed object within the tail, rather than a flat graphic applied to it. The effect connects a familiar circular landmark with technical depth, without implying better optical performance from its decorative structure.

Tactile richness is concentrated where bodies touch · Auto Express
CONTACT-LED MATERIALS

Tactile richness is concentrated where bodies touch

Alfa specifies soft Poltrona Frau nubuck in direct-contact zones rather than distributing it across every interior surface. This places tactile investment at seats and touch areas, while surrounding structural volumes keep a more technical character. Perceived quality is concentrated at meaningful contact, offering a material-allocation strategy rather than blanket premium coverage.

Brand geometry survives a technical reinterpretation · Auto Express
TELEPHONE WHEELS

Brand geometry survives a technical reinterpretation

The forged wheels reinterpret Alfa's telephone pattern through six circular elements arranged across three spokes. Openings and triangular grooves lighten the visual structure while repeating the Scudetto motif at a smaller scale. A brand landmark migrates between components through related geometry; this visual analysis is separate from any claimed aerodynamic benefit.

05 · What Zebra Can Apply

Four concrete experiments for Zebra products.

01 · Reduce information during engagement

Prototype a focused scanning state that removes secondary menus after the trigger is pressed, borrowing Cuorerosso's concentrated-instrument principle. Compare completion time and missed alerts; preserve essential exception feedback.

02 · Keep tactile confirmation at the grip

Give scan, confirm and release controls distinct shapes, using the Magma Display's physical anchors as inspiration. Evaluate blind identification with gloves, vibration and one-handed use before replacing mechanical controls.

03 · Spend material value at contact zones

Allocate softer, replaceable materials to strap and palm-contact regions while keeping impact surfaces structurally purposeful. Compare pressure discomfort, cleaning resistance and service time before extending premium finishes across the whole device.

04 · Make a changing mode physically legible

Use a moving or tactile indicator to confirm trigger or cradle mode changes, inspired by the living instrument housings. Check state recognition without staring at the screen and after repeated attachment cycles.

06 · Key Takeaway

Electrification can change the powertrain while physical feedback preserves the relationship.

Cuorerosso’s strongest design argument lies in the interface: moving instruments concentrate attention, while rotary and toggle controls retain tactile cues. The transferable lesson for Zebra is to reduce information at moments of high engagement while preserving reliable, reachable feedback at the grip.

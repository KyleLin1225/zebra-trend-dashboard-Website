01 · Design Highlights

A grand tourer combines low visual mass with a welcoming cabin.

A narrow cabin sits within broad shoulders · Auto Express
FELINE STANCE

A narrow cabin sits within broad shoulders

RCZ Vision contrasts a slim glass canopy with strong front and rear shoulders. The body feels planted while the glazing appears lighter, creating a muscular silhouette without relying on sharp aggression across every surface. The result reads as a composed electric GT, with softer transitions tempering intensity rather than uniform sharpness across the body.

A four-seat concept keeps the touring brief visible · Auto Express
ACCESS AND PACKAGE

A four-seat concept keeps the touring brief visible

Peugeot describes RCZ Vision as a dedicated four-seater with rear seatbacks that fold to increase luggage space. The open-door view places access and seating within the low coupe package, rather than treating the rear as a purely symbolic gesture. Sportiness supports touring utility; actual entry clearance and adult accommodation remain manufacturer claims rather than verified photo measurements.

Rear occupants are part of the spatial story · Auto Express
GRAND TOURING CABIN

Rear occupants are part of the spatial story

The rear seating image shows individual positions within the continuous glazed cabin and a calm light-blue material environment. The surrounding surfaces extend the front cabin's welcoming character, giving the GT proposal more than a driver-only visual emphasis. Shared travel feels included in the brief, while real comfort and visibility depend on geometry rather than the image’s generous impression.

Support is deliberately asymmetric · Auto Express
SEAT ARCHITECTURE

Support is deliberately asymmetric

Peugeot describes seats with greater support toward the vehicle centre, both for lateral support and to protect the floating central display. Open sections in the seatbacks reduce visible mass while retaining a shaped supporting structure. Ergonomics and display protection are considered together; the source does not publish measured comfort or impact results.

02 · Technology / Product Architecture

Electronic control and transparent displays reorganise the driver’s work.

Primary functions stay near the hands · Auto Express
HYPERSQUARE

Primary functions stay near the hands

Hypersquare's four rings place important functions within fingertip reach while the essential-information display sits above the control. Peugeot combines this interface with steer-by-wire that changes steering response according to speed, reducing repeated hand movement during manoeuvres. Continuous control is separated from optional information; adaptation and task testing remain necessary before assuming equal benefits for every driver.

Information can recede without removing essential status · Auto Express
TRANSPARENT MICRO LED

Information can recede without removing essential status

RCZ Vision uses transparent displays that can be configured to reduce the visible flow of information. The central display can disappear visually when switched off, while a separate display above Hypersquare keeps necessary driving data in view. The architecture distinguishes continuous from optional data; transparency alone is not evidence of lower distraction.

Important settings keep distinguishable hardware · Auto Express
PHYSICAL CONSOLE

Important settings keep distinguishable hardware

The floating centre console groups machined aluminium controls on a frosted-glass surface. Peugeot retains physical access to functions including driving modes and climate settings, while the digital displays handle configurable information. Tactile anchors remain inside an electronic environment, offering distinguishable actions rather than making every function an interchangeable touchscreen tile.

Battery thickness becomes a proportion enabler · Peugeot · Official imagery
SOLID-STATE PACKAGE

Battery thickness becomes a proportion enabler

Peugeot proposes a 110-millimetre solid-state battery and a 1,000-volt architecture for RCZ Vision. The compact battery package is linked to the exceptionally low silhouette and targets of 500 hp, 600 km range and rapid charging. Architecture enables proportion, while the 500 hp, 600 km range and charging figures remain proposed concept performance.

03 · Aesthetic Analysis

Glass, horizontal claws and warm materials temper technical intensity.

A familiar signature changes its direction · Auto Express
HORIZONTAL CLAWS

A familiar signature changes its direction

RCZ Vision reinterprets Peugeot's three-claw lighting motif as thin horizontal elements beneath a compact headlamp. The repeated lines stretch the face and give the wide body a precise technical rhythm without covering it in unrelated graphics. A brand signal changes orientation while remaining related to its heritage, without implying superior beam performance or road-user safety.

The tail keeps its graphic and surface systems aligned · Auto Express
REAR FLUIDITY

The tail keeps its graphic and surface systems aligned

The rear claws continue across a smooth, sculpted tail rather than interrupting the body with large separate lamp housings. Peugeot also describes an active spoiler that remains hidden when stationary and deploys while driving. The static object stays visually continuous while motion serves use; the image does not prove quantified aerodynamic improvement.

A physical timepiece links object craft to digital space · Auto Express
SERICA DETAIL

A physical timepiece links object craft to digital space

Peugeot integrates a SERICA Parade watch into the support for the central display. Its physical design is echoed by a digital interpretation, connecting a crafted object with a configurable information surface rather than treating them as competing experiences. The crafted object gives scale and warmth to the interface, without implying that an analogue clock inherently improves driving safety.

Soft and machined materials remain visibly different · Auto Express
WARMTH AND TECHNOLOGY

Soft and machined materials remain visibly different

RCZ Vision pairs light-blue Alcantara and recycled-leather upholstery with machined aluminium and glass details. The contrast gives hand-contact objects a precise technical reading while larger soft surfaces make the cabin appear warmer and calmer. The image supports sensory contrast; environmental benefit and wear resistance require evidence beyond the concept’s material descriptions.

05 · What Zebra Can Apply

Four concrete experiments for Zebra products.

01 · Keep critical actions physically locatable

Prototype distinct scan and confirm controls near the grip, taking Hypersquare's fingertip organisation as a reference. Compare blind identification, glove performance and mis-taps against the current handheld layout.

02 · Reduce secondary information during work

Keep scan status and urgent exceptions visible while allowing secondary interface content to recede, inspired by transparent-display hierarchy. Evaluate gaze shifts and missed alerts during repeated warehouse tasks.

03 · Coordinate ergonomics with component protection

Review grip, trigger and display relationships together, borrowing the asymmetric seat's combined support-and-protection logic. Check contact pressure, accidental strikes and screen accessibility in normal carrying and scanning postures.

04 · Give material contrast an operating role

Use distinct textures for touch controls and surrounding housings, following the console's soft-versus-machined contrast. Test cleaning, wet-hand identification and glove recognition before choosing finishes for visual refinement alone.

06 · Key Takeaway

A calmer cockpit depends on selective information, not the disappearance of every physical control.

RCZ Vision pairs configurable transparent displays with fingertip controls and a tactile centre console. Zebra can apply the hierarchy by keeping continuous status visible, reducing secondary screen clutter and preserving identifiable physical actions under gloves, vibration and time pressure.

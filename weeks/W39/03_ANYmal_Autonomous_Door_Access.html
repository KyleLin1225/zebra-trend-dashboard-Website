05 · What Zebra Can Apply

Treat every controlled threshold as a service-design moment.

01 / DIGITAL IDENTITY

Give devices governed credentials

Treat scanners, robots and accessories as managed identities with role-scoped access and revocation.

02 / THRESHOLD HMI

Make authorization state legible

At a blocked passage, show request, grant, deny and fallback states to worker and remote operator.

03 / RETROFIT SYSTEM

Design around installed infrastructure

Provide APIs and physical mounting strategies that work with existing doors, readers and plant workflows.

04 / FAILURE RECOVERY

Plan the denied-access path

Make safe stop, retry, human intervention and audit trail part of the product experience.

06 · Key Takeaway

A robot’s route is only as autonomous as the doors along it.

ANYmal pairs robot identity with existing LEGIC and dormakaba access systems, then checks that the passage is clear. Zebra can apply the same sequence to mobile workflows: authenticate at the point of action, show authorization and safety status, and surface a usable exception path.

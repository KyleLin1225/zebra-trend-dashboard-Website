Design Trend Report · 2026-W39

Five lenses on interaction, fit and field systems.

21–27 September 2026 · Select a topic to open the full report.

01 · AUDIO WEARABLES

Ray-Ban Meta Audio

Ray-Ban Meta Audio omits the camera while retaining open-ear audio and voice interaction. For Zebra, the transferable questions are how workers recognize sensing states, how they adjust fit during long shifts, and how a headset remains useful without visual capture.
02 · IMMERSIVE COMPUTING

Meta VR Glasses

Meta VR Glasses shifts power and processing to a pocket puck while keeping the head-worn display light. Zebra should test the resulting cable routing, holster, clip, donning time and repair path as one system when exploring service and training displays.
03 · INDUSTRIAL ROBOTICS

ANYmal Door Access

ANYmal pairs robot identity with existing LEGIC and dormakaba access systems, then checks that the passage is clear. Zebra can apply the same sequence to mobile workflows: authenticate at the point of action, show authorization and safety status, and surface a usable exception path.
04 · OUTDOOR WEARABLE

Amazfit T-Rex Dual Solar

Dual-sided solar charging and low-power recovery reframes power as an outdoor workflow.
05 · CAPTURE-ENABLED AI EYEWEAR

Ray-Ban Meta (Gen 3)

Revised imagery ties the three frames, physical control, front sensing modules and real wear contexts to specific Zebra interaction and fit tests.

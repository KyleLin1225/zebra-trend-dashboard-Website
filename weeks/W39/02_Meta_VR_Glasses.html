05 · What Zebra Can Apply

Translate the split system into wearable tools that remain comfortable through a shift.

01 / WEAR COMFORT

Move weight off the head

For a warehouse display or wearable scanner, place battery and compute at a belt or vest point, then test cable snag and neck load.

02 / HANDS-FIRST HMI

Design for occupied hands

Use gaze to target and a small hand action to confirm; retain clear feedback and a reliable fallback when gloves or motion interfere.

03 / FIT & SERVICE

Treat fit parts as service parts

Make nose supports, prescription inserts and cable interfaces easy to fit, clean and replace across users.

04 / SYSTEM CMF

Design the whole wearable kit

Coordinate the eyewear, tether, puck and clips as one durable system with a visible cable path and consistent touchpoints.

06 · Key Takeaway

Moving compute off the face changes the whole wearable system.

Meta VR Glasses shifts power and processing to a pocket puck while keeping the head-worn display light. Zebra should test the resulting cable routing, holster, clip, donning time and repair path as one system when exploring service and training displays.

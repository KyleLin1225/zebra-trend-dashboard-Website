05 · What Zebra Can Apply

Use the camera-free split to rethink wearable communication at work.

01 / PRIVACY SIGNAL

Make audio state obvious

For worker-facing wearables, pair microphones with legible mute and recording-state feedback.

02 / FIT RANGE

Design adjustment into the kit

Offer swappable pads and adjustable temple tips that can be cleaned and replaced between shifts.

03 / HANDS-FREE HMI

Use voice selectively

Reserve voice for high-value commands and offer a tactile fallback in noisy or privacy-sensitive areas.

04 / AUDIO SYSTEM

Keep ears aware

Test open-ear prompts against ambient alarms, machinery noise and hearing protection.

06 · Key Takeaway

Clear sensing boundaries make work eyewear easier to trust.

Ray-Ban Meta Audio omits the camera while retaining open-ear audio and voice interaction. For Zebra, the transferable questions are how workers recognize sensing states, how they adjust fit during long shifts, and how a headset remains useful without visual capture.

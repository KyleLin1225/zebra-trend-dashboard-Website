05 · What Zebra Can Apply

Test energy fallback as a worker workflow.

POWER RECOVERY

For Zebra field devices, test an emergency energy mode that restores only scan confirmation, location and a safe handoff workflow before normal operation.

OFF-DEVICE CHARGING

Explore whether a dock, holster or exposed back surface can offer opportunistic energy recovery without asking a worker to change grip or wearable position.

GLANCE STATES

Translate route/status hierarchy into short, high-contrast operational states for outdoor technicians; validate readability with gloves, glare and motion.

ENVIRONMENTAL LIMITS

Treat low-temperature operation and water exposure as explicit interaction states, with legible feedback when sensing, charging or connectivity is constrained.

06 · Key Takeaway

Design for the moment a field device runs out of power.

Amazfit pairs front and rear solar collection with an ultra-low-power recovery mode and essential route functions. For Zebra, the useful design experiment is a recoverable minimum service state with visible charging guidance and a tested handoff when a remote worker has no outlet.

01 · Design Highlights

Make a change of mode understandable.

Singapore Airlines Next-Generation Cabins · Divider defines the mode · mainlymiles.com

Divider defines the mode

The First Class centre pair can be used as separate suites or opened into a shared space, as the photographed divider makes plain. A movable partition changes privacy before the seats move together into a two-person sofa or bed. Make individual and shared states legible.

Singapore Airlines Next-Generation Cabins · Controls close to the hand · mainlymiles.com

Controls close to the hand

The Business Class panel groups several seat and lighting actions into a small physical control surface near the occupant. The visible pictograms and repeated button shapes make adjustment possible without reaching for the main entertainment screen. Test icons and accidental presses while reclined.

Singapore Airlines Next-Generation Cabins · Dining needs a second posture · mainlymiles.com
First · companion use

Dining needs a second posture

The First Class photo shows two people facing each other across a table inside a single suite. Companion dining gives the cabin a different social geometry from sleeping or solo work without replacing the seat shell. Keep controls reachable across postures.

Singapore Airlines Next-Generation Cabins · Leg support is a mechanism · mainlymiles.com
Business · reach

Leg support is a mechanism

The Business Class close-up exposes the movable leg rest and the area beneath the seat. Its travel changes both supported posture and the clearance for entering or leaving a reclined space. Test pinch points and cleaning access.

02 · Technology / Product Architecture

Coordinate controls, memory and service access.

Singapore Airlines Next-Generation Cabins · One saved position · mainlymiles.com
First · recall

One saved position

A finger presses a dedicated star control on the First Class console in the photographed detail. Singapore Airlines says smart cabin controls can remember preferred seat positions and lighting across journeys in First and Business Class. Show the proposed movement before recall.

Singapore Airlines Next-Generation Cabins · Information beside the seat · mainlymiles.com
First · local display

Information beside the seat

The First Class side panel shows flight and destination information on a small embedded touch surface. A local secondary display can answer a short question without taking over the large entertainment interface. Keep essential actions available offline.

Singapore Airlines Next-Generation Cabins · Storage becomes an edge · mainlymiles.com
Business · pocket

Storage becomes an edge

The Business Class side pocket and adjacent work surface are visibly integrated around the seated user. That placement makes small personal items reachable without placing them on an active control panel or seat cushion. Secure and clean the accessory zone.

Singapore Airlines Next-Generation Cabins · Actuation and repair · mainlymiles.com
Business · RECARO

Actuation and repair

A RECARO development image shows a technician beside the new Business Class seat mechanism. RECARO attributes its noiseless actuation and wide service access to this Business Class product, not to the First Class centre-suite conversion. Keep quiet motion accessible for repair.

03 · Aesthetic Analysis

Use materials to distinguish private and shared zones.

Singapore Airlines Next-Generation Cabins · A soft private boundary · mainlymiles.com
First · material zoning

A soft private boundary

The solo First Class suite pairs a warm seat, side cabinet and sliding privacy surfaces in a continuous enclosure. These materials distinguish body contact from storage and circulation without making every face look like the same plastic panel. Cue grip, storage and service zones.

Singapore Airlines Next-Generation Cabins · Contrast marks the seat · mainlymiles.com
Business · shell

Contrast marks the seat

The Business Class seat is framed by a dark shell, pale cushions and a subtle highlighted edge. The contrast separates where a passenger rests from the enclosing structure, helping a complex space read as a few clear zones. Test durability and cleaning before adoption.

Singapore Airlines Next-Generation Cabins · A wider shared baseline · mainlymiles.com
Premium · ergonomics

A wider shared baseline

The Premium Economy row shows side bolsters, head support and a distinct armrest for each seated passenger. The geometry offers personal contact zones in a dense cabin where full privacy walls would be impractical. Allocate support before enlarging displays.

Singapore Airlines Next-Generation Cabins · Keep the dense row legible · mainlymiles.com
Economy · consistency

Keep the dense row legible

The Economy row repeats a clear seatback screen, tray and lower pocket across several adjacent positions. A repeated interface grammar lets a new occupant understand the same actions at any seat in the row. Keep scan and charging locations consistent.

Give small items a home

The Business Class storage close-up shows a dedicated slot below the primary work surface. Zebra could add a drainable stylus or battery pocket by a vehicle dock. Test gloved retrieval and dusty cleaning.

06 · Key Takeaway

Let one setting travel with the user.

The new First Class centre pair converts from two private seats into a shared sofa or bed, while First and Business Class controls can recall preferred positions and lighting. RECARO separately describes noiseless actuation and service access for its Business Class seat. Zebra can translate these mechanisms into clearly signalled task modes, recoverable user preferences and maintainable shared-device hardware.

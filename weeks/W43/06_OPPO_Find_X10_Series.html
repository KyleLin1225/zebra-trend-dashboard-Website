OPPO Find X10 Series

A Hasselblad-engineered camera system and a factory-built creator rig turn the Find X10 series into two different products depending on which accessories a user chooses to attach.

01 · Design Highlights

Camera architecture and creator hardware share one language

Camera island detail · www.oppo.com
Camera island architecture

A sculpted island carries the Hasselblad mark

The Find X10 Pro Max raises its triple 200MP cluster into one squared island, separating lens glass from a satin body panel. OPPO and Hasselblad brand the seam directly on the glass rather than in a logo badge elsewhere, treating the camera module as the phone's primary identity mark.

Khronos creator kit · www.youtube.com
Khronos grip geometry

A cage adds hand controls the phone lacks

Tilta's Khronos cage wraps the Pro Max in a metal frame, adding a side grip, a top handle and cold-shoe mounts the bare phone has no room for. The added bulk trades one-hand pocketability for the two-hand stability a shoulder-height video shot actually needs.

Luna White finish · www.oppo.com
Split-glass finish

One back panel, two deliberate textures

Luna White pairs a velvet-etched glass upper panel with a smooth glass lower half, meeting in a visible seam below the camera island. The split reads as a design choice rather than a manufacturing limit, giving the matte and gloss zones distinct grip and fingerprint behavior.

Colorway range · www.oppo.com
Colorway range

Three finishes aim at three moods

The base Find X10 ships in Glacier Blue, Glow Coral and Mist Grey, while the Pro Max narrows to Titanium Grey and Luna White. Pairing a brighter, younger palette on the standard model with quieter tones on the flagship tier signals price and positioning before a spec sheet does.

02 · Technology / Product Architecture

Optics, rigging and cooling form one capture system

Teleconverter kit · www.oppo.com
Teleconverter optical path

An external lens extends the internal one

The clip-on Hasselblad Teleconverter sits in front of the 200MP periscope lens rather than replacing it, adding roughly 3.28x magnification on top of the phone's own zoom range. Keeping the native telephoto as the optical foundation lets OPPO sell reach as an accessory instead of a redesign.

Khronos rig mechanism · www.oppo.com
Modular rig mechanism

One mount accepts lens, light and fan

The Khronos cage exposes a cold-shoe and a filter thread so the teleconverter, an on-camera light and a vented cooling fan can all attach to the same chassis. Routing every accessory through one mechanical interface keeps the phone's own housing unmodified while still accepting professional video hardware.

Dimensity 9600 Pro · www.oppo.com
Compute thermal path

A 2nm chip still needs a heat path

The Pro Max pairs its 2nm Dimensity 9600 Pro with a vapor-chamber and cryo-velocity cooling stack sized for 8K video rather than burst benchmarks. Sustained recording is a thermal problem before it is a silicon one, which is exactly why Tilta's add-on fan exists as a second line of defense.

Enco X4 in use · www.oppo.com
Cross-device pairing

Earbuds that remember two separate devices

Enco X4 holds simultaneous connections to two devices across Android, iOS and Windows, so switching from phone to laptop skips the usual re-pairing step. For a creator filming on the Find X10 and editing elsewhere, that persistent dual link removes a small but constant workflow interruption.

03 · Aesthetic Analysis

Form language stretches from pocket phone to rig

ProXDR bezel · www.oppo.com
Bezel symmetry

A near-invisible frame around the display

The Pro Max's 1.15mm bezel runs nearly even on all four sides, so the ProXDR panel reads as one continuous surface rather than glass set into a frame. That symmetry is a display achievement independent of the camera system the rest of the body is built around.

Teleconverter in use · www.oppo.com
Rig vs. pocket form

A pocket object becomes a stage prop

Caged in the Khronos rig with its grip, filter thread and lens barrel, the same phone reads as concert-photography equipment rather than a consumer handset. The transformation is entirely additive: nothing about the Find X10's own shape changes, only what gets bolted around it for the shoot.

Titanium Grey finish · www.oppo.com
Titanium Grey surfacing

A quieter metal reads as the senior finish

Titanium Grey trades the Luna White split-texture story for one uniform brushed-metal impression, a common cue for the more expensive or more serious option in a lineup. Next to Luna White's two-texture glass, the plain metal finish does the positioning work a price tag usually has to do alone.

Enco X4 · www.oppo.com
Cross-category CMF echo

Earbuds borrow the phone's soft palette

Enco X4's Glacier White shell and rounded case echo the phone line's pale, cool colorways and soft-gradient product photography rather than introducing a separate audio-accessory look. Keeping the finish language consistent across categories is what makes a phone, earbuds and tablet read as one family on a shelf.

05 · What Zebra Can Apply

Translate OPPO's add-on thinking into Zebra hardware

Reference for review service paths early01

Review service paths early

OPPO's X-ray view of Enco X4's driver stack is a review format worth copying: render Zebra's compact modules the same way early in design reviews to catch serviceability problems before tooling is locked in.

Reference for design grab-and-go companions02

Design grab-and-go companions

Pad Mini's slim, one-hand carry makes it a grab-and-go companion, not a device to plan around. Apply that logic to a companion tablet a Zebra worker can grab beside a handheld without a spare holster.

Reference for borrow the tap-to-pair gesture03

Borrow the tap-to-pair gesture

OPPO's Touch to Share uses physical proximity, not a menu, to start a transfer between two devices. A tap-to-pair gesture between a handheld and a sled or printer would cut setup steps during warehouse handoffs.

Reference for give modes a visual identity04

Give modes a visual identity

Hasselblad's XPan mode gets its own color accent and framing guide inside OPPO's camera app, legible at a glance. Zebra's scan and data-entry modes could use the same distinct treatment to cut mode-confusion errors.

06 · Key Takeaway

Accessories, not the phone, decide what the Find X10 becomes.

OPPO ships one phone chassis and lets Hasselblad optics, the Tilta Khronos rig and ColorOS features decide whether it behaves as a pocket camera or a rigged video tool. For Zebra, the transferable idea is the same: design one rugged core and let bolt-on modules — grips, fans, sleds — define the use case, rather than building a separate device for every job.

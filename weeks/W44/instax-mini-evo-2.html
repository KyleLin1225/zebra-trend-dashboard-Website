Make capture and print one clear sequence.

Keep state visible

The product image links live capture to a visible printed result. Zebra should make ready, preview, queued and printed states unmistakable, then test whether operators can recover from a wrong selection before media is spent.

06 · Key Takeaway

A preview can prevent a wasted print.

The mini Evo 2 makes framing, capture, review and printing distinct moments through a screen and physical controls. Zebra can use the same separation in field labeling and evidence capture: preview the exact output, confirm the destination, then commit media only when the operator is ready.
